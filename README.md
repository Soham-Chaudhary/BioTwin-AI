## Problem It Solves
People ignore small daily habits until serious health issues appear.  
No system shows you **“This is what your body will look like in 5 years”** if you continue the same lifestyle.

## Solution
**BioTwin AI** creates a **digital twin** of your body that:
- Learns your current habits
- Simulates your health 1–5 years into the future
- Shows organ-level impact
- Gives clear “what-if” recommendations

> **“We don’t just predict disease — we simulate how your lifestyle reshapes your body over time.”**

## Features
- Real-time Digital Twin Dashboard with risk gauge
- Organ-Level Impact Analysis (Heart, Lungs, Brain, Metabolism)
- Digital Twin Interpretation & Insights
- Future Trajectory Graph (Current vs Improved Lifestyle)
- Simple lifestyle text input with smart keyword detection

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Streamlit
- **ML Model**: RandomForestClassifier (scikit-learn)
- **Simulation Engine**: Mathematical time-series projection
- **Visualization**: Plotly
- **Data**: Synthetic behavioral dataset modeled after real health trends
